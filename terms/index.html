1. What the App does — and what it doesn't promise

Waker is an alarm clock that keeps re-ringing until your iPhone's camera has verified a morning mission: push-ups, squats, a plank hold, or a photo of a household object the App picks at random when the alarm goes off. An unfinished mission stops ringing on its own after about 30 minutes.

It is a convenience and motivation tool, not a safety-critical system: do not rely on it as your only safeguard for situations where oversleeping could cause harm or loss. Alarms depend on your device being on, charged, with the ringer volume up and alarm authorization granted — conditions the App checks where it can but cannot guarantee. Because iOS gives the ring the system's own volume, it can be turned down with the hardware buttons; the App cannot prevent that.

2. Health disclaimer

The exercise missions are exercise. The App provides no medical advice. Consult a professional before starting an exercise routine if you have any condition that makes exercise risky, and stop if something hurts. You can set any mission to as little as one repetition, or choose the object-photo mission, which asks for no exercise at all.
