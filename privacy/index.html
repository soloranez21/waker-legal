Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Waker is an alarm app that verifies your morning mission with your iPhone's camera. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

The short version

  • Your camera data never leaves your iPhone. Mission verification runs entirely on-device. No photo, video, or depth data is ever recorded, stored, or uploaded — frames are processed in memory and immediately discarded.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no account to delete.
  • The alarm works offline. Scheduling, ringing, missions, streaks, and the escape hatch make zero network calls.
  • We don't track you. No advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking, no data sales. Ever.

Data stored on your device

Alarm settings, mission history, streaks, and escape-pass usage are stored locally on your iPhone (and in your device backups, which Apple encrypts per your iCloud settings). Deleting the app deletes this local data. The limited paywall personalization details described below are also processed by Superwall.

Data processed by third parties

Purchases (Superwall + Apple). Our subscription paywall is powered by Superwall, and payments are handled by Apple's App Store. When you view the paywall or purchase, Superwall receives standard purchase and paywall-interaction events tied to a device-level identifier (Apple's "identifier for vendor"). We have no accounts, so there is no email or account identity to send.

Paywall personalization. If you gave a first name during setup, we send it to Superwall for personalized paywall messaging. When a paywall is shown, we also send your selected morning goal and preferred morning activity, if provided. The onboarding paywall can additionally receive your planned wake-up time and chosen mission type. These choices help the paywall show the morning plan you selected; they may also appear in Superwall's paywall and placement analytics, linked to the same device-level identifier.

We do not send your age range, sleep-habit answers, feelings, snooze history, owned-object list, or actual mission results for this personalization. We do not send camera images, video, depth, or movement data. Choosing “I’m not sure yet” uses general goal messaging; it does not infer a goal for you.

This is used to personalize and operate the paywall and measure it, not for cross-app advertising or data sales. See Superwall's privacy policy (superwall.com/privacy) and Apple's (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Apple processes your payment; we never see your payment details.

Permissions we ask for and why

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): to schedule and ring your wake-up alarms, including the backup alarms that re-ring until your morning mission is done.
  • Notifications: for the late backup nudges if you keep skipping the mission.
  • Camera: to run the wake-up mission — counting pushups, squats or a plank hold, recognising the household object you were sent to find, or verifying visible brushing-like hand-at-mouth motion. On-device only, as above.

You can revoke any of these in Settings at any time; the app will tell you what stops working.

Children

Waker is not directed at children under 13.
